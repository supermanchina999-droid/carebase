The Problem

Play Therapists spend 15 minutes writing session notes after every 30-minute play session. That's 1-2 hours of documentation per day. And SimplePractice/TherapyNotes only have talk therapy note templates.

Play Therapists need to document:
• Which toys were used and how
• Play behaviors observed (pretend play, aggressive play, withdrawn, etc.)
• Emotional responses during the session
• Developmental milestones reached
• Parent-friendly progress reports
